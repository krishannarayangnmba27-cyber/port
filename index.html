BRANDS & DIVISIONS MANAGED
VIVEL SAVLON SUPERIA ENGAGE YIPPEE NOODLES (₹5 Drive)
1. CONTEXT
Market Landscape & Objectives

Undertaken as part of the MBA curriculum at ITC's PCP division in Rajgarh, Alwar. The objective was to diagnose retail distribution gaps, understand retailer reluctance, and tackle the recurring issue of underperforming stockists lapsing into the inactive "Bronze" tier.

2. MY CONTRIBUTION
Field Operations & Strategy

Conducted daily in-person retail market visits, performed territory outlet mapping, executed structured surveys with shopkeepers to identify credit/delivery issues, tracked order fulfillment with distributor logistics, and pushed slow-moving SKUs (₹5 Yippee) using bundled discount schemes.

3. EVIDENCE
SIP Project & Frameworks

Flagship Project Report: “Reduction in Bronze Stockists: Enhancing Sales Performance of ITC’s PCP Products” (Enrollment: 25SOM2039000019). Survey tools, stockist tier matrices (Gold, Silver, Bronze), and daily sales visit records submitted and approved by ITC Area Executive.

4. OUTCOME & LEARNING
Key Insights & Personal Growth

Learned that delivery delays and lack of guaranteed sell-through were the primary drivers of stockist lapse. Proved that tighter distributor delivery synchronization paired with micro-incentives significantly accelerates retail reordering. This solidified my passion for channel sales and retail analytics.

FIELD METHODOLOGY & STEP-BY-STEP IMPLEMENTATION
01
Outlet-by-Outlet Territory Mapping: Mapped retail stores across Rajgarh, categorizing counters into actively stocked versus irregular/non-stocking outlets.
02
Structured Retailer Surveys: Diagnosed specific barriers including distributor visit frequency, distributor credit terms, shelf-space competition, and localized brand preferences.
03
Performance Tiering: Classified accounts into Gold, Silver, and Bronze tiers based on order frequency and average basket size to prioritize follow-up visits.
04
Order Follow-Through & Incentive Schemes: Tracked deliveries end-to-end with the local distributor to prevent stock-outs, utilizing bundled schemes to push trial packs.

SEO & Digital Visibility Analysis of Lenskart Using Ubersuggest

Context: Digital & Social Media Marketing Coursework • Omnichannel Eyewear Industry
Context & Approach:

Analyzed Lenskart's omnichannel digital acquisition engine using Ubersuggest. Audited organic search traffic, keyword ranking profiles, domain authority, search intent distributions, and backlink networks.

INDEPENDENT STRATEGIC ANALYSIS

Strategic Moats & Market Disruption: Patanjali Ayurved vs Microsoft

Context: Strategic Management Case Study • FMCG Disruption vs Enterprise Tech Ecosystem
Context & Approach:

Conducted a dual-industry comparative evaluation examining how market entry strategies differ between FMCG consumer goods (Patanjali) and global software enterprises (Microsoft). Applied Porter's Five Forces and VRIO models.

Evidence & Findings:

Patanjali disrupted incumbent FMCG players via cultural positioning, pricing advantages, and dedicated distribution stores. Microsoft maintained leadership via high enterprise switching costs and ecosystem integration.

Recommendations & Learning:

Low-cost disruption requires continuous supply-chain quality control to sustain early wins; enterprise lock-in relies on continuous platform synergies. Learned how distinct competitive moats shape longevity.

INDEPENDENT MARKET ANALYSIS

Competitor Mapping & Positioning: Beyond Farms in D2C Agri-Food

Context: Brand Selection & Market Mapping Assignment • Organic Food Market
Context & Approach:

Mapped direct and indirect competitors in the premium organic food and farm-to-fork D2C ecosystem. Evaluated pricing tiers, packaging strategies, retail shelf placement, and customer acquisition channels.

Evidence & Findings:

Found severe saturation in basic organic staples but high margin potential in value-added cold-pressed items and subscription boxes with traceable farm origins.

Recommendations & Learning:

Recommended transparent farm-to-table traceability campaigns and tiered loyalty programs to drive repeat subscription orders and elevate customer lifetime value (LTV).
